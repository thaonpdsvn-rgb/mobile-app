Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Lumigem ("the App", bundle / package com.boxapp.lumigem) handles your information. The App is developed and operated by an individual developer ("we", "us"). Contact: thaonp.dsvn@gmail.com.

1. No account, local game data

The App does not require an account or sign-in. Your progress, stars, coins, gem styles and settings are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App. All pictures are built into the App; it does not download content from our own servers.

2. Data collected by third-party services

The App uses the following third-party SDKs, which may collect limited data automatically:

  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent): advertising ID (IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking), device and OS information, IP address (approximate location) and ad interaction data, used to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: pseudonymous app instance ID, device information and in-app events (for example a picture started or finished), used to understand how the game is played and to improve it.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash logs and device state at the time of a crash, used to fix bugs.
  • RevenueCat with Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase: purchase history and a pseudonymous app user ID, used to deliver and restore purchases. We never receive your payment card details; payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, files or microphone audio.

3. Advertising, consent and tracking

The App never shows ads while you are playing a picture. It may show an interstitial ad between pictures and offers an optional rewarded video to double the coins of a finished picture. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings (privacy options), when that option is available for your region. If you do not consent, the App tells Google Analytics and AdMob not to use your data for personalized advertising.

On iOS, the App asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency before using the advertising identifier. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized with that identifier. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

Purchasing Remove Ads or the Complete set turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded videos are always optional.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data security and retention

Data sent by the SDKs above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data held by Google and RevenueCat is retained according to their own policies. Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the App.

6. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access or delete data held about you. Because the App has no accounts, the identifiers involved are your advertising ID and pseudonymous app instance / purchase IDs. You can reset the advertising ID in device settings. To request deletion of data held by our service providers, email thaonp.dsvn@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
